# Risk Band Decision Matrix

| Risk Band | Suggested Action | Owner | SLA | Governance Control |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Low | Continue standard flow | Operations | Real time | Monitor aggregate drift |
| Medium | Monitor or sample | Quality analyst | Same shift | Sampling review |
| High | Prioritize inspection | Quality manager | Before next production stage | Log decision and override if needed |
| Critical | Escalate for mandatory review | Quality lead | Immediate | Mandatory human review and audit log |

Risk bands support prioritization. They should not be used as fully automated rejection decisions without additional validation and formal approval.
