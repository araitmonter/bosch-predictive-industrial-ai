# Board Presentation Outline

## 1. Executive Thesis

Predictive quality AI can improve manufacturing resilience when paired with clear governance, monitoring, and human accountability.

## 2. Business Problem

Manufacturing failures create rework, scrap, warranty exposure, and operational disruption. Traditional inspection does not always prioritize components by risk.

## 3. AI Opportunity

Use production-line measurements to score component-level failure risk and route components to pass, monitor, inspect, or escalate decisions.

## 4. Data Architecture

Present the medallion architecture from sensors and test stations through Bronze, Silver, Gold, model registry, scoring API, dashboards, and monitoring controls.

## 5. Data Readiness

Show the executive scorecard: completeness, consistency, traceability, predictive readiness, governance readiness, and monitoring readiness.

## 6. Predictive AI Model

Explain the model as a risk-scoring system using class imbalance handling, threshold optimization, and false-negative management.

## 7. Explainability

Show global and local explanations. Emphasize that anonymized features must be mapped to real stations before operational deployment.

## 8. Executive Dashboard

Demonstrate KPIs, risk score distribution, decision bands, high-risk components, monitoring indicators, and strategic recommendations.

## 9. AI Governance Framework

Review model card, data card, operating model, approval requirements, monitoring requirements, and human-in-the-loop policy.

## 10. Risk Register

Discuss false negatives, false positives, drift, data quality degradation, lack of explainability, feature anonymity, operational misuse, and governance gaps.

## 11. 90-Day Roadmap

Outline station mapping, data lineage, shadow scoring, threshold tuning, monitoring baselines, and controlled pilot approval.

## 12. Final Recommendation

Approve a governed pilot for inspection prioritization. Defer autonomous production control until performance, explainability, and operational controls are proven.
